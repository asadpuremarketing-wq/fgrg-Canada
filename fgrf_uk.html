Our Mission

Faizan Global Relief Foundation (FGRF) is a non-governmental organisation dedicated to creating lasting, positive change by providing community-led, holistic solutions. Our work focuses on empowering people and transforming lives through a wide range of initiatives designed to address the most pressing challenges of our time.

Our Vision

Our efforts extend across the globe, supporting communities in some of the most challenging environments. Whether it’s providing emergency relief after a natural disaster or helping communities build self-sufficiency, (FGRF UK) is there to offer hope and support when it’s needed most.

Our comprehensive approach ensures we meet the diverse needs of the communities we serve, fostering self-reliance and promoting sustainable progress for future generations.

FGRF’s Global Reach and Impact

Our efforts extend across the globe, supporting communities in some of the most challenging environments. Whether it’s providing emergency relief after a natural disaster or helping communities build self-sufficiency, FGRF is there to offer hope and support when it’s needed most.

Our comprehensive approach ensures we meet the diverse needs of the communities we serve, fostering self-reliance and promoting sustainable progress for future generations.

Pure Water

Faizan Global Relief Foundation UK (FGRF UK) is delivering life-changing water projects across regions, including Thar Desert, Balochistan, and Punjab in Pakistan, as well as several countries in Africa.

Medical & Health

FGRF is dedicated to improving health outcomes by organising medical camps, offering free consultations, and delivering critical healthcare services to those in need.

Healthy Food

We distribute food packages to those facing hunger and malnutrition, ensuring that no one is left without basic necessities.

Education

We provide educational opportunities and advanced technical training to help individuals acquire new skills, build careers, and create brighter futures.

Join Us in Making a Difference

FGRF’s mission is only possible thanks to the support of people like you. With your help, we can continue to provide vital services to those who need them most. Whether you’re looking

to donate, volunteer, or simply learn more about our work, we invite you to join us in making a meaningful impact on communities around the world.

Together, we can create a better future for all.

FGRF adopts a simple yet impactful approach to charitable giving, firmly rooted in the

principles of Sharia. Our model ensures that contributions remain affordable and deeply rewarding, empowering individuals to support those in need by donating just a few pence a

day. This accessible approach enables everyone to take part in meaningful change, regardless of their financial circumstances.

Türkiye & Syria Earthquake Relief Efforts (2023)

On 6th February 2023, a devastating earthquake struck Türkiye and Syria, claiming over 70,000 lives and leaving countless families in urgent need of support. In response, Faizan Global Relief Foundation (FGRF) acted swiftly. Our teams held an emergency coordination meeting and boarded the earliest available flight to deliver aid on the ground.
Donate Now

Pakistan Flood Relief Efforts

Faizan Global Relief Foundation (FGRF) responded swiftly and effectively to the devastating floods in Pakistan, delivering critical aid to the communities most affected. Our dedicated representatives and volunteers worked in close coordination with government officials to provide both immediate relief and long-term recovery support.

Morocco Earthquake Relief – A Lifeline in Crisis

In September 2023, a powerful earthquake struck Morocco, claiming over 3,000 lives and leaving thousands more in shock and urgent need of support. In the immediate aftermath, Faizan Global Relief Foundation (FGRF) mobilised its emergency response teams to provide critical aid to those affected.

Faizan Global Relief Foundation (FGRF), in collaboration with Dawat-e-Islami, is working across His Majesty’s Prisons (HMP) in the United Kingdom to bring hope, guidance, and meaningful transformation to those behind bars.

Fighting Against Thalassemia

Faizan Global Relief Foundation (FGRF) is deeply committed to supporting individuals affected by thalassemia and other blood-related illnesses, particularly vulnerable children. During the COVID-19 lockdowns, an alarming shortage of blood supplies put countless lives at risk. In response, FGRF stepped up and successfully collected over 86,000 bottles of blood, directly saving and improving lives.

COVID-19 Response Efforts by Dawat-e-Islami

Dedicated COVID response teams were established throughout the UK, comprised of passionate volunteers determined to help those most affected.
